## Coordinated CH022 + CH026 topology

First simultaneous attempt:
[39158101675](https://github.com/C31N/nbfm1-open-bci/actions/runs/39158101675).
The pinned CH022_N_ELECTRODE has **zero pre-existing vias**.
Therefore a valid CH022 B.Cu detour cannot use a previous same-net
via in the J2.23 area. A correct alternative needs two *new* vias,
each in a physically legal, F.Cu-connected local via window,
one for R302.1 and the other near J2.23. The original
J2.23 tracks remain entirely preserved.

The revised model
[CH026/CH022 joint workflow](https://github.com/C31N/nbfm1-open-bci/actions/workflows/ch026-ch022-joint.yml)
places legal via points only after verifying their individual
connected F.Cu windows, proposes R302 and J2 F.Cu branches, then
attempts a B.Cu return and restores the previously selected CH026
segment(s). The native model must still prove the complete
electrical connectivity and native DRC, not just geometry.
