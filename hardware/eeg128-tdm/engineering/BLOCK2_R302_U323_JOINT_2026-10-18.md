# R302.1 and U3.23 coupled Block 2 routing experiment

Based on immutable diagnostic 137 geometry and the verified 139-airwire
engineering checkpoint. Branch: codex/block2-r302-u323-joint-2026-10-18.

## Evidence-based baseline

A pinned native KiCad read-only survey (run 39154318423) measured:
- CH022 J2.23 at (43.75, 7.6) mm: nearest modeled new via is 1.15 mm
- CH022 R302.1 at (36.15, 15.6) mm: nearest modeled new via is 3.385149 mm
- CH024 U3.23 at (34.785, 35.85) mm: nearest modeled new via is 2.327571 mm

The two selected CH022 segments were at least 6.1 mm and 6.319217 mm
from J2.23. The joint trial separately verifies **all** original
CH022 tracks and vias within 0.75 mm of J2.23 remain byte-identical
before considering the trial. VCM and CH018_N_MUX remain untouched.

## Coordinated mesh reservation

With the exact prior eleven-conflict copper scope and the existing
CH019-P via bridge, six native-hosted models attempt both route orders,
using 0.35, 0.65, or 0.95 mm radius F.Cu reservations around the
opposing critical pad. CH022_N is rooted at R302.1 in first-order
trials and J2.23 in last-order trials. The first net reserves the
second net's approach rather than taking the narrow corridor.

This is geometrical diagnostic only: all removed neighboring tracks
must be fully restituted and the joint net topology proven native
before any proposal is eligible for acceptance. Exact 0.505 mm new
via/In2 exclusion from complete L3-power polygon, 0.25 mm A2 board
edge clearance, exact 0.0 mm2 L3 loss/gain, zero errors/warnings,
zero net regressions and schematic parity are hard gates.
Independent byte-identical PCB+ZIP replay and at least 70 native
hardware safeguards are mandatory for any eventual 136 checkpoint.
No fabrication Gerber, Drill, CPL or BOM export is allowed.

The scripts always set accepted=false, routing_completed=false and
fabrication_release=false. No primary .kicad_pcb is changed.

GitHub Actions [39155025519](https://github.com/C31N/nbfm1-open-bci/actions/runs/39155025519)
ran six identical-baseline, immutable-VCM/CH018 models. All completed
their diagnostic jobs, but none completed joint routing.

| First net | Reserved opposing pad on F.Cu | Radii tested | First unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CH022-N | U3.23 | 0.35 / 0.65 / 0.95 mm | CH024-P at U3.23 |
| CH024-P | R302.1 | 0.35 / 0.65 / 0.95 mm | CH022-N at R302.1 |

With CH024 first, all three CH024 pads and the existing-via CH019 bridge

The two selectively removed CH022 F.Cu segments are at 6.1 and
6.319217 mm from J2.23, respectively.

## Corrected local via access to R302.1

A preliminary forced-via test mistakenly selected the same global
new-via candidate (44.1186,16.9) mm for both CH022 pads because it
did not constrain windows to the corresponding pad's local F.Cu
connected component. That led to a degenerate zero-length route
which was correctly rejected. These results are **invalid as physical
route proposals** and were not promoted.

The corrected [39156232333](https://github.com/C31N/nbfm1-open-bci/actions/runs/39156232333)
model independently requires a legal new via center within 3.5 mm
of each pad *and* reachable from that pad's current F.Cu free
component. All four runs return:

- R302.1: **0.00000000 mm²** local, F.Cu-reachable legal new-via area.
- CH022-first: CH022 blocked at R302.1 before CH024 is attempted.
- CH024-first: the complete three-pad CH024 geometry and CH019 bridge
  are proposed, then CH022 remains blocked at R302.1.
- All J2.23 original escape tracks/vias, CH018-N, VCM, and L3-power
  protected outlines remain unchanged. All trial reports retain
  accepted=false and fabrication_release=false.

This proves the specific **short local new-via detour near R302.1**
is not legal on this immutable neighbor geometry. It does not prove
a more distant, existing-via or jointly displaced-neighbor solution
is impossible. Any next candidate must explore an existing CH022 via
or a fully restorable local F.Cu neighbor relocation, without
touching J2.23.

## Engineering gating decision

No full-neighbor-restored 136 native board exists. Candidate DRC=0/0,
137→136 airwire decrease, exact 0.0 mm² L3 gain and loss, independent
byte-identical PCB+ZIP replay, parity and all native release guards
have **not** been jointly demonstrated. No PCB in the mainline/checkpoint
was modified, and no Gerber, NC-Drill, CPL or BOM files were exported.
Keep:
- accepted=false
- routing_completed=false
- fabrication_release=false
- confirmed original 139 unconnected
