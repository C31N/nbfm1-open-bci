# R302.1 and U3.23 coupled Block 2 routing experiment

Based on immutable diagnostic 137 geometry and the verified 139-airwire
engineering checkpoint. Branch: codex/block2-r302-u323-joint-2026-10-18.

## Evidence-based baseline

A pinned native KiCad read-only survey (run 39154318423) measured:
- CH022 J2.23 at (43.75, 7.6) mm: nearest modeled new via is 1.15 mm
- CH022 R302.1 at (36.15, 15.6) mm: nearest modeled new via is 3.385149 mm
- CH024 U3.23 at (34.785, 35.85) mm: nearest modeled new via is 2.327571 mm

The two selected CH022 segments were at least 6.1 mm and 6.319217 mm
from J2.23. The joint trial separately verifies **all** original
CH022 tracks and vias within 0.75 mm of J2.23 remain byte-identical
before considering the trial. VCM and CH018_N_MUX remain untouched.

## Coordinated mesh reservation

With the exact prior eleven-conflict copper scope and the existing
CH019-P via bridge, six native-hosted models attempt both route orders,
using 0.35, 0.65, or 0.95 mm radius F.Cu reservations around the
opposing critical pad. CH022_N is rooted at R302.1 in first-order
trials and J2.23 in last-order trials. The first net reserves the
second net's approach rather than taking the narrow corridor.

This is geometrical diagnostic only: all removed neighboring tracks
must be fully restituted and the joint net topology proven native
before any proposal is eligible for acceptance. Exact 0.505 mm new
via/In2 exclusion from complete L3-power polygon, 0.25 mm A2 board
edge clearance, exact 0.0 mm2 L3 loss/gain, zero errors/warnings,
zero net regressions and schematic parity are hard gates.
Independent byte-identical PCB+ZIP replay and at least 70 native
hardware safeguards are mandatory for any eventual 136 checkpoint.
No fabrication Gerber, Drill, CPL or BOM export is allowed.

The scripts always set accepted=false, routing_completed=false and
fabrication_release=false. No primary .kicad_pcb is changed.
