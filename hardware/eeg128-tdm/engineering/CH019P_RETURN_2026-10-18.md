## New search strategies

1. Try only the failing segment, failing plus immediate neighbor, and
   all three CH019-P In2 segments in separate disposable KiCad projects.
2. Measure legal through-via centers around each removed segment endpoint,
   reporting nearby 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 and 3.5-mm windows. The protected power
   region is the *complete In2.Cu L3-zone outline* buffered by 0.505 mm.
3. After a complete three-pad CH024_P_MUX model path, reroute CH019-P
   either through all existing F.Cu pad terminals (alternate F.Cu/B.Cu
   access, no forced via at old In2 segment endpoints), or along the old
   In2 endpoints as a control. Then reroute CH022-N pad terminals.
4. If all geometric proposals succeed, inspect the *temporary* PCB using
   a fresh KiCad 8 DRC and diff connected nets, L3 power polygons
   (exact zero loss and gain), and zone outlines against the baseline.

The pathfinder uses a native board polygon routing domain with at least
0.25 mm A2 clearance plus track/via radii. All through-vias and new In2
paths observe the 0.505 mm L3 exclusion. Native DRC is authoritative.

All reported variants always set:
`model_only=true`, `accepted=false`, `routing_completed=false`,
`fabrication_release=false`, `independent_replay_verified=false`.
**No candidate may be promoted** until exact 136 native unconnected,
zero DRC errors/warnings, CH024+CH019+CH022 truly closed, no connectivity
regression, 0.0 mm² L3 gain and loss, unchanged board/rules, schematic
parity, independent byte-identical PCB+ZIP replay and all 70 hardware
safety tests are verified. No Gerber, Drill, CPL or BOM export.

## F.Cu/B.Cu pad-root and operation-order experiment

The initial bounded-domain native-hosted models demonstrated that selective
single/double In2.Cu releases cannot open CH024-P U3.23. Full three-segment
removal opens all three CH024-P pads, while endpoint reconstruction fails
at (41.75, 31.579999) to (43.25, 33.079999). A *pad-driven* attempt
starting at R295.2 also fails reaching CH019-P endpoint U3.6 **after**
CH024-P is routed. This is not a fully restored 136 candidate.

The next probe tests U3.6 as the CH019-P routing root, in two operation
orders (after or before CH024-P), plus R39.1 as an alternative root before
CH024-P. Complete CH022-N connectivity remains mandatory. All results
remain `accepted=false`; a native DRC and independent replay are required
before any eventual checkpoint.
