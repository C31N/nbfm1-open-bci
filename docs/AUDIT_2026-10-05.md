## EDA findings

### Schematic

The current `hardware/eeg128-tdm/eeg128-tdm.kicad_sch` is a KiCad 8 S-expression document, but it does not contain instantiated electronic symbols and wires. It explicitly delegates detailed endpoint connectivity to `NETLIST.csv`.

That file is therefore **not a fabrication-grade schematic** and shall not be represented as one.

### PCB

The current PCB source contains the component placement and pad-to-net assignment for the A0 review layout, but no completed copper routing, vias or copper zones.

Accordingly:

- `routing_completed=false`
- `fabrication_release=false`
- `human_connected_use_authorized=false`

must remain false.

### ADS131M08 package audit

The ADS131M08IPBSR uses TI package drawing **PBS**, a 32-pin TQFP. TI's mechanical drawing identifies a nominal 5 mm square body, 0.50 mm lead pitch, and approximately 7 mm outer lead span. The BOM description `TQFP-32_5x5mm_P0.5mm` is therefore not rejected merely because TI's product page also describes the package envelope as 7 mm × 7 mm.

Authoritative package verification shall use the TI PBS mechanical drawing, not only distributor metadata.

References:

- https://www.ti.com/product/ADS131M08
- https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m08.pdf
- https://www.ti.com/packaging/docs/carriermaterial/carrierlookup?OPN=ADS131M08IPBSR&state=details

### Grounding conflict

The design intent says the four-layer board should use a continuous return plane, but the logical source presently distinguishes `AGND` and `DGND`.

Before routing release, one explicit, reviewed grounding strategy is required:

1. a single continuous ground net/plane with analog/digital partitioning by placement and return-current control; or
2. an explicitly documented net-tie strategy whose location and impedance are reviewed.

No arbitrary ground-plane moat is permitted.

### Reference filtering

The design uses the ADS131M08 internal reference. TI characterizes the converter with a 100 nF capacitor at REFIN for internal-reference operation. TI support also notes that the capacitor filters bandgap noise and may be omitted for some modes/use cases.

For the low-noise EEG target, the A0 release review shall explicitly decide and document the REFIN network rather than leaving it as an accidental NC.

## Hardware release conditions

The A0 board shall not be exported as a fabrication release until all of the following are independently completed:

1. complete KiCad schematic with symbols, pins and electrical connectivity;
2. complete four-layer copper routing;
3. ground strategy resolved;
4. manufacturer land-pattern verification for every non-generic footprint;
5. BOM/CPL/PCB reference and orientation cross-check;
6. KiCad 8 ERC pass;
7. KiCad 8 DRC pass, including schematic parity;
8. Gerber/drill visual review;
9. isolated, current-limited bench bring-up without a human subject;
10. input-noise, settling, crosstalk, CMRR and fault-condition measurements;
11. safety review under `docs/SAFETY_COMPLIANCE.md`.

## Legal audit

### United States

35 U.S.C. §102(a)(1) can treat a claimed invention as prior art where, before the effective filing date, it was patented, described in a printed publication, in public use, on sale, or otherwise available to the public.

The statute also contains exceptions, including certain inventor-originated disclosures under §102(b). No repository statement should claim a universal automatic bar against every later U.S. patent claim.

Official source:

https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section102

### European Patent Convention

Article 54(2) EPC includes everything made available to the public by written or oral description, use or any other way before filing. EPO guidance states that there is generally no geographic or language restriction, and documentary prior art must contain an enabling technical teaching.

Official sources:

- https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/a54.html
- https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/g_iv_1.html
- https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/g_iv_2.html
- https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/g_iv_7_5.html

## Licensing audit

This repository is **not** “dual licensed” in the usual sense of offering the same work under two alternative licences.

It is a **path-scoped multi-license repository**:

- `hardware/**`, `firmware/**`: CERN-OHL-S-2.0
- host/model/privacy/deployment/documentation paths: AGPL-3.0-only

The two domains may coexist in one repository and interoperate through documented interfaces. That does not establish universal licence compatibility if source from both licences is copied into one derivative work.

The CERN OHL project identifies CERN-OHL-S as the strongly reciprocal v2 variant:

https://cern-ohl.web.cern.ch/

## Regulatory and safety audit

There is no blanket “FDA Class II/III exemption”.

FDA states that most Class I and **some** Class II devices are exempt from 510(k), subject to the classification regulation and other controls. Investigational device studies are separately governed by the IDE framework and risk classification.

Relevant official sources:

- https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/classify-your-medical-device/class-i-and-class-ii-device-exemptions
- https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/investigational-device-exemption-ide/ide-approval-process

EU MDR applicability depends materially on intended purpose and other scope provisions. Calling a repository “research only” does not override the actual intended purpose, labeling, promotion or use.

## Final audit rule

**Software publication readiness and PCB fabrication readiness are separate release gates.**

The software/defensive-publication repository can be publicly released while the physical A0 PCB remains explicitly unreleased for fabrication and prohibited for human connection.
